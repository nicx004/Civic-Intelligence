# CivicIntelligence

## Overview

CivicIntelligence is an AI-powered citizen-to-government grievance routing and resolution support system.

The system accepts unstructured citizen complaints, extracts structured information, identifies responsible departments, maps complaints to municipal jurisdictions, and generates standardized action tickets while informing citizens about expected resolution timelines.

---

## Problem Statement

Municipal corporations receive thousands of citizen complaints through portals, helplines, emails, WhatsApp bots, and social media.

Common challenges include:

- Unstructured complaint descriptions
- Missing information
- Incorrect department routing
- Multi-department complaints
- Lack of transparency
- Delayed resolutions

Example:

"Road flooded near Whitefield Metro Station and streetlights are not working."

This complaint spans:

- Drainage Department
- Electrical Department

Traditional systems often fail to handle such cross-domain complaints efficiently.

---

## Vision

Create an intelligent complaint processing pipeline that:

1. Understands citizen complaints
2. Requests missing information
3. Converts complaints into structured data
4. Maps complaints to municipal jurisdictions
5. Routes them to responsible departments
6. Provides SLA-based response timelines
7. Maintains transparency for citizens
